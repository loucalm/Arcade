Every enemy is a **musical instrument fused with a creature, driven mad by the Sourdine** (a stain of silence); hitting it retunes it and it bursts into coins. This sheet will be cut automatically by a script, so the grid must be perfectly regular.

## Style
- Keep each enemy's main colours and instrument from the attached scale image, but make them **much cooler**: stronger silhouettes, big expressive **white eyes** (the shared signature of all enemies), grumpy/crazy detuned expressions, cute and chunky, mischievous not scary, no gore. Keep the **same facing as the current sprite**.
- Light from the top-left, 3 tones per material, 1-pixel dark outline #14101C, highlights on metal, wood and drum skins. Saturated colours that stand out on busy backgrounds.
- Original designs only: do not imitate any existing game.

## THE SHEET (exact layout, very important)
- Flat **#FF00FF magenta background** everywhere. No text, labels, numbers, grid lines, ground or shadows.
- **640 × 832 game pixels**: **13 rows of 64 px**, each row made of **10 cells of 64 × 64**, filled from the left (unused cells stay magenta). Draw it at **×2 (1280 × 1664 image)** if you can, otherwise ×1. Every game pixel a clean square, no anti-aliasing, no gradient, no blur. Nothing crosses a cell border.
- Grounded enemies: centred, **feet or base on the bottom row of the cell**, 40-54 px tall. Flying things: centred in the cell.
